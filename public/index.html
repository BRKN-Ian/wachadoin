Onboarding a new employee

  1. Go to Team Members and add them with a name, email, and a temporary password (this is just their dashboard login — most staff never need to use it day-to-day).
  2. Click Agent Key next to their name. You'll see a download link, a Server URL, and a personal Agent Key.
  3. Send those three things to the employee (Slack, email, WhatsApp — whatever's convenient) and have them run the installer on their own computer.
  4. The one-time setup screen asks for the Server URL and Agent Key — once they paste those in and click Connect, it's done. The agent then runs quietly every time their computer starts; no daily login, ever.
  5. Before switching monitoring on for someone new, make sure they've had the POPIA notice / consent form — see the reminder at the bottom of this page.

Offboarding — when someone leaves

Go to Team Members and click Deactivate next to their name. This immediately:

  • blocks their dashboard login, even if they're already logged in;
  • stops the background agent from reporting anything further from their computer;
  • keeps their name and past time/activity records on file — nothing is deleted.

If you ever need their record gone entirely (e.g. a data-erasure request), use Delete permanently instead — but that's a one-way door, so Deactivate is the right first move in almost every case. Made a mistake? Reactivate restores their access.

How Monitoring Rules matching works

Wachadoin checks each employee's currently active window — the app name plus whatever's in its title bar (for a browser, that's usually the page or site name) — against the short words/phrases you've added under Work-related and Red flags. It's a simple "contains" check, not case-sensitive: adding xero matches "Xero – Dashboard — Google Chrome" and would also match anything else with "xero" in it.

For an accounting firm, a sensible starting point (or just click "+ Add suggested accounting-firm list" on that page):

  • Work-related: xero, simplepay, payspace, caseware, draftworx, pastel, sars efiling, outlook, teams
  • Red flags: youtube, facebook, instagram, tiktok, zoho, sage — plus job sites like indeed, pnet, linkedin jobs (useful for spotting morale issues early, not just distraction)

Quick answers

Someone shows "Offline" and never changes — check the agent is actually installed and running on their computer (Team Members → Agent Key has the download links again).

Before you start monitoring staff

Wachadoin captures screenshots, active-window titles, and activity levels from employees' computers. Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), this counts as processing employees' personal information, so a few things need to be in place before you switch it on:

1. Tell your employees

Give each employee written notice of what's monitored (screenshots, app/window titles, idle time), why, who can see it, and how long it's kept. Have them sign an acknowledgment.

2. Update employment contracts

Add a monitoring clause to new and existing employment contracts so consent is documented on file, not just a one-off form.

3. Limit and secure the data

Only collect what you need for legitimate work-performance purposes, restrict dashboard access to managers who need it, and don't repurpose screenshots for anything unrelated to work performance.

Acute Accountants can provide a staff consent form and suggested contract clauses to get this in place — this notice isn't legal advice, so have a lawyer review the final wording for your business.
