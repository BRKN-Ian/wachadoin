Privacy-law aware from the ground up

Monitoring staff computers counts as processing personal information under most data-privacy laws — South Africa's POPIA, the EU/UK GDPR, and similar laws elsewhere. Wachadoin's dashboard walks managers and partners through the notice-and-consent steps before switching monitoring on for anyone, and every account gets a plain-language reminder of what's required.
